# Right Lane privacy policy

Version of 5 October 2026.

## In short

Right Lane collects no personal data. No account, no ads, no usage statistics, no trackers.

## 1. Controller

Guillaume Vanden Herrewegen (Belgium), publisher of the app.

Contact: [contact@right-lane.be](mailto:contact@right-lane.be)

## 2. What stays on your phone

The app saves a few settings on your phone:

- the text size;
- the colours (dark or light theme);
- the language you chose;
- the fact that you've read the welcome screen;
- the fact that you've closed the message about the new Highway Code;
- a copy of the terms of use and of this policy, so you can read them offline.

This information never leaves your phone. Nobody, not even the publisher, has access to it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## 3. Internet connections

All the app's content (rules, legal texts, images) is already in the app. It works offline.

The app only connects to the internet in two cases:

- to download the latest version of the terms of use and of this policy. These texts are hosted by GitHub (GitHub, Inc.). Like any website, GitHub then receives your phone's IP address. The publisher has no access to it. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement);
- when you tap a link (Belgian Official Gazette, Wikimedia Commons…). The website then opens in your browser and follows its own privacy policy.

## 4. If you write to the publisher

If you send an e-mail (to report a mistake, for example), the publisher receives your e-mail address and your message.

They are only used to reply to you and to correct the app. They are never sold or shared. They are kept for as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted.

Legal basis: the publisher's legitimate interest in replying to messages and correcting the app (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 5. Downloading the app

If you install the app from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple process data (account, download) under their own privacy policy. The publisher only receives anonymous statistics (number of downloads, app crashes).

## 6. Children

The app can be used by children: it collects no data, about anyone.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of the data about you (for example an e-mail you sent), or object to its processing. Write to [contact@right-lane.be](mailto:contact@right-lane.be).

You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority: rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, [www.dataprotectionauthority.be](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be).

## 8. Changes

This policy may change. The current version is always shown in the app and on [this page](https://right-lane.be/en/privacy.html), with its date.

The French version of this policy is the reference version.
